Keyboard and build notes
  • Ring and wheel: ← → move 5°, Shift moves 45°. You can also tap, drag or scroll, or use the − / + buttons. On phones, a flick keeps the wheel spinning until friction stops it on a notch. With reduced motion turned on, it stops where you let go.
  • Grid: the arrow keys move around, Enter picks, and Esc clears.
  • The ring and the phone wheel are the same role="slider" with two presentations. Only one renders at a time, so screen readers never hear two hue controls.
  • ToggleGroup for the format, NumberField for − / +, and Toast for copy confirmations.
